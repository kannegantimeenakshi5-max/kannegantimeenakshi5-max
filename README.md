## What I work on

- **Structure-based drug discovery:** docking with AutoDock and analysis of protein-ligand interactions
- **Homology modelling:** building and validating 3D protein models with SWISS-MODEL
- **Cheminformatics and ML:** drug-likeness screening and potency prediction on public bioactivity data (RDKit, ChEMBL)
- **Small interactive tools** in Python and Streamlit

## Research experience

- **InhA inhibitors for *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*:** docked phytochemicals against InhA (PDB 4TRN) with AutoDock, identified Puddumin B as a lead compound benchmarked against isoniazid, and presented the work at the **BioCloud Conference (2024)**.
- **Homology modelling of rhamnogalacturonan lyase:** built models from 8+ organism sequences, with over 88% of residues in favoured regions (validated with RAMPAGE and PROCHECK). Manuscript in preparation.
- **Currently:** a guide-supervised in silico drug-discovery project on *Leishmania donovani* protein targets (in progress).
